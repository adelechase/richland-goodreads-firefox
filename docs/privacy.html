Privacy information

Applies to Richland Library on Goodreads, version 2.5.1. Updated November 11, 2026.

The extension reads the title, author, and, when available, ISBN of a book on Goodreads. It sends identifying search information to Richland Library's catalog, its OverDrive/Libby collection, and Hoopla to check availability. These services may process ordinary website requests under their own privacy policies.

Hoopla lookups may use temporary hidden Firefox tabs and the Hoopla session you're already signed into. The extension does not request, read, store, or transmit your library card number or Hoopla password. It closes its own temporary search tabs after they finish or time out.

The extension does not operate a developer server, use analytics or advertising trackers, or intentionally keep a history of books you've viewed. Third-party services may maintain their own request logs.

Firefox categorizes the book information read from the Goodreads page as websiteContent, which is why the extension declares that data collection permission.

For complete details, see the full privacy policy in the repository.
